3A characteristic: Right facing bovines, bold, angular, curved, script deeply engraved.
Script irregular, angular.

3A Doubtful (3AD): Right facing animal (may be 3A - but doesn't provide contextual information)

Transitional 3A/3B Carving styles : Engraving not angular but smooth and rounded; incised and hatched lines
finer suggesting finer tips, sharper tools.

3B characteristic: Edges are smoother and rounded - compared to sharp/angular of 3A. Unicorn is adorned
more, greater number of incised or hatched lines. Smaller features such as ear, eye etc are more finely
engraved. Overall proportion of body changes.
Script deeper, broader than 3A. Usually placed at the top of the seal. Orientation more linear cf 3A. Longer!!
(means more products :)). Often irregularly spaced; sometimes extend towards the animal motif.
